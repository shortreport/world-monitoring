European Commission publishes its final 2027 Work Program
By Sonja Rijnen · Nov 4, 2026, 1:28 PM · View in your browser

BRUSSELS — The European Commission on Wednesday published the final version of its 2027 Work Program outlining its policy priorities for the year ahead.

The document is a “strategic, targeted and streamlined work program, doubling down on our political priorities and main work streams,” Trade Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič told reporters.

He said the key policy priorities for next year include “competitiveness and energy prices” as well as “responding to the demands of Europeans and European businesses.”

A draft version of the European Commission’s work program was leaked to POLITICO on Tuesday; the final version contains some differences.

The final document says the Commission will “explore the possibility” of introducing a clean heat market mechanism. Tuesday’s leaked draft said it would put forward the mechanism.

The final program also adds a line on cybersecurity that was absent from the draft, saying: “The EU will strengthen Europe’s collective cyber preparedness, resilience and industrial competitiveness in a streamlined manner.”

Specific references on reinforcing the European firefighting fleet were removed in the final version from the section on adaptation and mitigating the effects of a changing climate. Instead, it says the EU will consider reinforcing “European capacities and capabilities.”

The final version also replaces the draft’s reference to efforts to bring about a “ceasefire” in the Black Sea with a commitment to “ensure freedom of navigation.”

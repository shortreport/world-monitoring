Document: Commission to propose industrial AI plan in 2027 work program
By Kathryn Carlson · Nov 3, 2026, 4:03 PM · View in your browser

The Commission will put forward a blueprint for industrial AI, it said in a leaked communication to its 2027 work program obtained by POLITICO.

The work program outlines the EU executive’s policy priorities for the year ahead. The full version is still to come.

“To tap the potential of artificial intelligence, we will propose a blueprint for action in five sectors of industrial AI: health, transport, agri-food, advanced manufacturing and defence and space, as well as actions to strengthen Europe’s position in the AI stack,” the document read.

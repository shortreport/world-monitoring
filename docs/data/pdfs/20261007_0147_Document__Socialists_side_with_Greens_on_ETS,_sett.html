Document: Socialists side with Greens on ETS, setting up battle with EPP
By Ben Makuch · Oct 6, 2026, 6:46 PM · View in your browser

The Socialists and Democrats —the second biggest group in the European Parliament — have sided with the Greens in pushing for tighter restrictions on industrial pollution under the Emissions Trading System, according to draft amendments obtained by POLITICO.

The position sets up a fight with the Parliament’s biggest group, the center-right European People’s Party, over how how far to go in weakening the bloc’s most powerful tool for bringing down planet-warming pollution.

Both the S&D and Greens reject the approach put forward by lead lawmaker and EPP member Peter Liese on the linear reduction factor — the percentage by which carbon permits are removed from the ETS.

Instead, they want to keep the LRF unchanged at a 4.4% annual decrease until 2035, and 2.7% after that. Liese and the EPP’s approach calls for a 3.4% LRF from 2031 to 2035 and then 2.3% from 2036 onwards.

The Commission proposed 3.7% from 2031 to 2035, then 1.7% from 2036 to 2040.

As with the Greens, the S&D calls for the deletion of the inclusion of international credits into the ETS — a European Commission and EPP endorsed proposal — and similarly requires that sectors under the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism be phased out of free carbon allowances by 2034.

The left-wing party, led on the ETS file by Dutch lawmaker Mohammed Chahim, also wants private jet owners and motorised private yachts brought under the ETS carbon-pricing regime, something not included in either the European Commission’s proposal or Liese’s draft report.

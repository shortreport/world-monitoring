Robert Kahn

+1 202.903.0011

About Robert Kahn

Robert's research focuses on macroeconomic policies and the transmission of shocks throughout the global economy. For much of his career he has also studied the resolution of sovereign debt crises and financial distress, including the role of debt restructuring. Prior to joining Eurasia Group, Robert was an economic consultant and adjunct professor at Johns Hopkins University. Before that, he was a senior fellow for international economics at the Council on Foreign Relations, a senior strategist with Moore Capital Management, and a senior adviser in the financial policy department at the World Bank, where he focused on financial sector assessments for developing economies and was the bank's liaison to the secretariat of the Financial Stability Forum. Robert has also held senior staff positions at the IMF and at Citigroup; he served as the head of the Office of Industrial Nations at the US Treasury and was a senior economist on the Council of Economic Advisers and the Federal Reserve Board.Robert received a bachelor's degree from the University of Chicago and a doctorate from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

Jeremy Chan

About Jeremy Chan

Jeremy Chan is a senior analyst on the China and Northeast Asia team at Eurasia Group. He focuses on Japan, the Korean Peninsula, and China's foreign policy, helping clients navigate political, economic, and security risks across northeast Asia. Jeremy previously spent a decade in East Asia, including as a US diplomat with tours in China and Japan, and with the SK Group in China and South Korea. Jeremy also worked for The Tony Blair Institute for Global Change in the Middle East, where he led a team of analysts in providing policy recommendations to senior government stakeholders. Jeremy holds a master's degree in China and Korea studies from the Johns Hopkins University's School of Advanced International Studies, including a year at the Hopkins-Nanjing Center, and a bachelor's degree in comparative literature from Princeton University.

Exclusive: High-Level Forums for Senior Leaders Navigating a Shifting World

Eurasia Group events convene policymakers, C-suite executives, investors, technology leaders, and civil society voices for strategic conversations that move beyond the headlines. Each forum combines public-stage dialogue with substantive off-the-record exchanges, creating space for candid discussion, informed debate, and the insights that shape consequential decisions. 

Those interested in participation are invited to contact rsvp@eurasiagroup.net or share the global events calendar with an appropriate colleague. Attendance is selective and thoughtfully curated to support focused, high-impact engagement. Eurasia Group works closely with participating organizations to identify the most relevant representatives based on the themes and priorities of each forum.

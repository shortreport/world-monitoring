CORRECTION: EU sets December deadline for military mobility deal
By Martina Sapio · Sep 28, 2026, 7:48 PM · View in your browser

EU countries and the European Parliament are aiming to reach a deal on the bloc’s military mobility regulation in December, according to a tentative timetable drawn up by the Irish Council presidency on Sept. 24 and seen by POLITICO.

After a first round of negotiations on July 16, the two sides are due to meet again on Oct. 20. EU ambassadors are expected to agree on the Council’s final negotiating position in early December, ahead of a final round of talks with Parliament on Dec. 9.

The military mobility regulation is designed to make it faster and easier to move troops, military equipment and supplies across the EU. It would simplify permitting and customs procedures, digitize paperwork, improve transport infrastructure, and create an emergency system for military movements during crises. It would also establish a “Solidarity Pool” of transport assets that countries could call on when needed.

Before the October and December political talks, officials from Parliament and national governments will continue working through the proposal in technical meetings.

The next session, on Oct. 1, will focus on the national coordinators responsible for military movements, as well as readiness checks and stress tests. Meetings on Oct. 9, 15 and 16 will cover transport rules and unresolved points in the text.

In November, talks will move to the Solidarity Pool, transport assets, infrastructure, governance, information security and data protection, with meetings scheduled for Nov. 5, 6, 9, 12.

Further sessions on Nov. 16, 18 and 20 will deal with remaining legal issues, including liability and international agreements.

The timetable remains tentative and could still change.

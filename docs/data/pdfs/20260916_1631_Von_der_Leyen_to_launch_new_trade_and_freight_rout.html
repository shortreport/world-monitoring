Von der Leyen to launch new trade and freight routes
By Jordyn Dahl · Sep 16, 2026, 9:30 AM · View in your browser

The European Union is launching a new connectivity project to link the South Caucasus and Central Asia through Europe as part of its push to diversify trade routes.

Commission President Ursula von der Leyen is set to unveil the project — dubbed Global Gateway — during her State of the Union speech on Wednesday, according to a transcript of her speech obtained by POLITICO ahead of time.

The Commission plans to raise more than €12 billion in public and private investments to fund the project, which will “diversify routes, triple trade flows and slash freight transit times by 2030.”

In the transcript, von der Leyen highlighted the project as one of many steps the EU is taking to bolster its trade relations in the wake of the global trade war started by U.S. President Donald Trump.

“In this age of tipping points, our prosperity and security will not only be built at home,” the speech reads.

The EU is organizing a Regional Connectivity Summit with Bulgarian Prime Minister Rumen Radev to launch the project.

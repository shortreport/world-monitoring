Romania blames Russia as military shoots down third drone
By Camille Gijs · Jul 26, 2026, 1:32 PM · View in your browser

A Romanian fighter jet on Sunday shot down a third drone that had entered its national air space in as many days, prompting President Nicușor Dan to blame Moscow for the “inadmissible and intolerable” incursions.

Sunday’s incident marked the latest escalation for the NATO member. The series of incursions comes as Russian attacks on Ukraine’s Danube region have increasingly spilled over the border.

“It is inadmissible and intolerable for the Russian Federation to continue violating Romania’s airspace, which is, at the same time, NATO airspace and European Union airspace,” Dan said on Sunday.

“Such actions are unacceptable, and we treat them with the utmost seriousness, alongside our allies,” he said on X.

The drone was safely shot down 12 kilometers northeast of Sulina, a port town in Eastern Romania on the Danube Delta, within the country’s territorial waters, the defense ministry said in a statement.

Romania’s foreign ministry said on Sunday that it had summoned Russia’s ambassador over the incursions.

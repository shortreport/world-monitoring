About Matias Gomez Leautaud

Matias is Eurasia Group's lead analyst for Mexico, advising clients on the political, regulatory, economic, and security developments shaping the country's business environment. With nearly a decade of experience across government, consulting, and political risk, Matias specializes in Mexico's energy sector, trade policy, elections, and the US-Mexico relationship.

Matias frequently represents Eurasia Group with commentary in international and Mexican media, including Bloomberg, the Financial Times, Reforma, El Universal, Milenio, La Hora de Opinar, and Nexos. He holds a bachelor's degree in international Relations from El Colegio de Mexico and a master's degree in public policy from the London School of Economics. Outside work, Matías is an avid NBA fan with the delusional conviction that next season is always the year the Los Angeles Lakers will claim their 18th championship.

About Antonio Ortiz-Mena

Antonio Ortiz-Mena is a senior consultant at Eurasia Group and President of AOM Advisors. A leading expert on North American trade, investment, and geopolitics, he was a member of Mexico’s NAFTA negotiating team and previously served as Minister for Economic Affairs at the Mexican Embassy in DC. His areas of focus include geopolitical, regulatory, and cross-border business risks. He is an Adjunct Professor at Georgetown University and chairs the USMCA Committee of Mexico’s Foreign Trade Council (COMCE).

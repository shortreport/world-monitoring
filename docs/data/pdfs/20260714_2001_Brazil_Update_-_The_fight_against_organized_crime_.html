Fabio Ramazzini Bechara is a Public Prosecutor in the State of Sao Paulo since 1996, currently designated to work at the Department of Transnational Organized Crime at the Organization of American States. At the Sao Paulo Public Prosecutor’s Office, he served as Executive Secretary to the Prosecutor General of Justice, Executive Secretary of the Special Task Force for Combating Organized Crime, Coordinator of the Technology Laboratory Against Money Laundering, and Coordinator of the Legislative Monitoring and Superior Courts Litigation Unit. He also coordinated plenty task forces that investigated and prosecuted Primeiro Comando da Capital (PCC) leadership.

Fabio is a Ph.D. in Criminal Procedural Law from the University of Sao Paulo (2010) and published books and articles on criminal law, international legal cooperation, illegal markets, corruption, and money laundering. At the State Secretariat for Public Security, he served as Coordinator of the Integrated Public Security Intelligence Center of the State of São Paulo, Secretary of the Integrated Action Agency Against Organized Crime, and Secretary of the State Forum for Combating Corruption and Money Laundering.
Bruna Santos is the director of the Brazil Program at the Inter-American Dialogue. She previously led the Brazil Institute at the Woodrow Wilson Center for Scholars. Prior to that, she served as vice president and director of innovation at Brazil’s National School of Public Administration (Enap), the country’s premier government training institution. Her work sits at the intersection of geopolitics, emerging markets, technology, and institutional strategy, with a focus on helping public and private-sector leaders navigate political risk, regulatory change, and long-term opportunities across Brazil, Latin America, the United States, and China.

Christopher's research focuses on comparative studies of national elections and he leads the firm's coverage of Brazil, and as part of the firm's research leadership, contributes to coverage of global geopolitical trends.

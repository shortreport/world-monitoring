About Derek Theurer

Derek Theurer is currently a partner at Venable LLP, where he advises clients on complex tax and economic policy matters, including federal tax legislation and regulations, global tax policy, financial services, and trade. He recently joined Venable from the U.S. Department of the Treasury, where he served concurrently as deputy undersecretary for legislative affairs, chief of staff, and deputy secretary, with responsibilities extended across the department's policy and enforcement portfolios. Derek led the Senate confirmation of presidential nominees, including other senior Treasury officials, the chairman of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve, and chairman of the White House Council of Economic Advisors. He previously advised the speaker of the U.S. House of Representatives on tax, trade, financial services, and retirement policy. As chief tax counsel at the Committee on Ways and Means, he served as the House’s senior technical tax advisor, counseling members and staff, and leading a team of lawyers and professional staff responsible for stewardship of the Internal Revenue Code. In that capacity, he worked across the aisle to develop and pass significant bipartisan retirement legislation in 2022.

About Jon Lieber

Jon Lieber is Eurasia Group's head of research and managing director for the United States. In these roles, he oversees the firm's global country and macro analysis and directs US political coverage. Before joining Eurasia Group, Jon worked for over a dozen years at the highest levels of the US government, including as an economic policy advisor for the House Ways and Means Committee, Senate Republican Leader Mitch McConnell, and in the National Economic Council at the White House. Jon has also worked as a tax policy consultant at a global accounting firm, an economist at a San Francisco-based tech startup, and a researcher covering monetary policy at the American Enterprise Institute. He holds a master's degree in economics and a bachelor's degree in philosophy from Tufts University. Jon lives in Washington, DC, with his wife and two children, and in his spare time enjoys being a permanent beginner student of music theory.

Parliament clears 7 EU budget files for negotiations
By Aude van den Hove · Nov 25, 2026, 1:17 PM · View in your browser

The European Parliament voted by large majorities to send seven proposals for the EU’s next long-term budget straight into negotiations with the Council, heading off a possible challenge from the far right.

The lawmakers were voting on the proposals for Global Europe, the European Social Fund, Horizon Europe, the Connecting Europe Facility, the European Competitiveness Fund, the asylum and migration program and AgoraEU.

These proposals are now moving to negotiations with the Council of the European Union based on reports adopted by the committees responsible for each file.

BYD in talks to acquire French manufacturing plant
By Jordyn Dahl · Nov 24, 2026, 4:12 PM · View in your browser

Chinese automaker BYD announced Tuesday it is in exclusive talks to acquire a car factory in Hambach, France, from Ineos Automotive, a British manufacturer of off-road vehicles.

“Our commitment to Europe is long term,” Stella Li, BYD’s executive vice president, said in a press statement. “Our proposed investment in Hambach reflects our confidence in France and our ambition to keep growing in Europe.”

The plant would help the automaker comply with new local content requirements that will be put in place through the EU’s Industrial Accelerator Act. Paris is a strong backer of the regulation and has advocated that only production in the 27 EU member states should qualify as Made in Europe.

Europe is a critical market for BYD and its Chinese peers, which are facing overcapacity in China and a weakening domestic car market.

BYD is a leader in mass market electric vehicles, along with producing its own batteries, but is also looking to challenge luxury brands BMW and Mercedes-Benz in Europe through its newly launched Denza brand.

BYD has already broken ground on a factory in Hungary that is expected to open next year. The Hambach plant would be its first purchase of a legacy European car factory.

Other Chinese carmakers are choosing to partner with European manufacturers, such as Leapmotor’s agreement with Stellantis that gives the manufacturer access to European plants and distribution networks.

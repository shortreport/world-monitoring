Introducing our new portal
 

Over the past couple of years, we’ve collaborated with many of you on how we can tailor our digital experience to better meet your needs. After months of development, we’ve officially flipped the switch: the new Eurasia Group portal is now live.

You will soon receive an invitation from library@eurasiagroup.net with the subject line “Welcome to the Eurasia Group Portal.”

When you receive it, please click “Activate my Account” to complete your first login. Your email address will be your username, and you’ll be prompted to set a new password the first time you sign in. Please note that your old password will not work in the new portal.

If you don’t see the invitation – or if you’re not receiving the usual email alerts when content is published – please check your junk/spam folder, and review your company’s email filtering settings. We also encourage you to proactively share the allowlisting instructions linked here with your IT team. 

We hope this new experience provides more intuitive access to our insights and our analysts. If you have any questions, please reach out to support@eurasiagroup.net.

Happy reading!

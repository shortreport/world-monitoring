Fuel trading among high-demand regions will prevent near-term shortages

By EG Energy, Climate & Resources, Henning Gloystein, Gregory Brew

China's reduced crude imports and cross-regional fuel trades are working in parallel to prevent near-term shortages across Asia and Europe, though global supply remains vulnerable to Chinese export restrictions and thin European distillate stocks.

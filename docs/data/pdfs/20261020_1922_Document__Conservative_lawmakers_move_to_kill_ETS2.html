Document: Conservative lawmakers move to kill ETS2
By Ben Makuch · Oct 20, 2026, 12:21 PM · View in your browser

BRUSSELS — Nine delegations of the European Parliament’s biggest group are calling for the second phase of the EU’s carbon pricing regime to be cancelled, according to draft amendments obtained by POLITICO.

The call from the center-right European People’s Party to end ETS2 comes as both Parliament and the Council of the European Union are reviewing a July reform of the Emissions Trading System.

ETS2, an update to the original ETS legislation, puts a new EU-wide carbon price on heating and transport fuels from 2028. The original ETS covers power plants and factories.

German EPP lawmaker Peter Liese is steering Parliament’s work on the Commission’s proposed reform to the ETS, which only amends the first ETS. But now the Polish, Spanish, Italian, French, Romanian, Croatian, Slovenian, Slovak and Estonian delegations of his own group are openly splitting with him in calling for ETS2 to be repealed as part of the review.

“ETS2 should be deleted,” the amendment, sponsored by Polish EPP lawmaker Adam Jarubas, argues. Another amendment in the same draft, backed by 10 delegations, takes a softer approach and asks for ETS2 to be “postponed until 2031.”

Liese did not propose touching ETS2 in his initial report on the ETS reform in September.

The move in Parliament follows similar moves from member countries. Last week at a meeting of EU leaders in Brussels, Poland and Italy successfully forced the European Council to call for the Commission to reassess the impacts of ETS2, in what many saw as a hijacking of discussions. Earlier in the day, both were part of a six-country alliance asking for ETS2 to be delayed until 2030.

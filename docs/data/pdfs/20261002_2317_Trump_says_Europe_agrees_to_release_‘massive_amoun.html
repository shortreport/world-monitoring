Trump says Europe agrees to release ‘massive amount’ of diesel
By Ben Munster · Oct 2, 2026, 4:16 PM · View in your browser

BRUSSELS — Europe has agreed to release stocks of diesel to offset rising prices, U.S. President Donald Trump said Friday, following a pressure campaign by the U.S. to get European countries to contribute.

“Europe has just agreed to release a massive amount of their heavily stocked Diesel Oil,” Trump wrote on Truth Social. “The process will begin immediately.”

The announcement comes after several rounds of emergency talks between European governments over how to respond to the U.S. pressure, with France proposing a release of 100 million barrels of European oil and diesel earlier Friday.

EU extends energy crisis state aid plan
By Francesca Micheletti · Nov 25, 2026, 3:10 PM · View in your browser

BRUSSELS — The European Commission will prolong the bloc’s temporary state aid measures to help companies hit by the ongoing energy crisis.

The Commission has decided to extend the Middle East crisis Temporary State Aid Framework untile June 30 next year, the EU executive announced on Wednesday.

The Commission launched METSAF in April to allow EU countries more leeway in supporting sectors suffering from the effects of the Iran crisis.

The framework will continue to cover agriculture, fisheries, land transport and shipping within the EU

For agriculture, fishery, land transport and shipping within the EU, member states will be allowed to compensate up to 70% of a beneficiary’s extra costs due to the price increase of fuel and fertilisers caused by the crisis.

The move comes in response to EU countries’ calls to prolong the framework, competition chief Teresa Ribera said, while cautioning that these remain temporary measures.

“Temporary support may help us navigate today’s crisis but only the transition to a clean economy and reduced dependence on volatile fossil fuel imports can shield us from tomorrow’s energy crises,” the commissioner said in a statement.

Commission President Ursula von der Leyen had anticipated the move at a European People’s Party offsite meeting last month.

So far, the Commission has approved 35 measures notified by 14 EU countries under the METSAF, as well as 11 amendments to existing projects The measures have an estimated budget of over €3.7 billion.

The Commission said it will continue to monitor and reassess the situation in spring 2027, when it stands ready to prolong the framework further.

Today's Key Takeaways
2026-10-29 13:23 JST  ·  ~1 min read

A major European pilots' union found that more than one-third of pilots are not getting adequate rest, raising safety concerns across the continent's aviation sector. The survey underscores growing fatigue problems in an industry already stretched thin by post-pandemic demand. This matters for automakers because supply chain disruptions from aviation delays or safety incidents would ripple through manufacturing, especially for companies reliant on air freight for critical parts and components. Watch whether European regulators tighten duty-time rules, which could slow logistics further.

China's CATL, the world's largest battery maker, is accelerating production line construction in partnership with Celenese as Beijing doubles down on advanced battery technology. The move signals intensifying competition in EV battery supply just as automakers globally are locking in battery sourcing deals for the next decade. The risk is real: if Chinese producers establish dominant manufacturing scale before Western competitors ramp up domestic capacity, pricing power and supply security could shift decisively eastward.

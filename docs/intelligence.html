Today's Key Takeaways
2026-10-27 10:38 JST  ·  ~1 min read

China's largest battery maker CATL is accelerating production capacity in partnership with a local producer, signaling confidence that Chinese EV makers will capture more of the global market even as Western automakers struggle with profitability in that region. The expansion comes as China doubles down on battery technology as a strategic priority, embedding supply chain control into its industrial policy.

This matters because battery costs determine EV margins everywhere. If CATL expands faster than Western competitors can match, Chinese automakers gain a structural cost advantage that compounds over time. European and American manufacturers already face tighter emissions rules and slower EV adoption at home—losing the battery cost race would squeeze them from both ends.

Watch whether European automakers use this week's earnings calls to signal price cuts or production cuts in response to Chinese competition.

Today's Key Takeaways
2026-07-23 07:00 JST  ·  ~1 min read

China's economy expanded 4.7 percent in the first half of 2026, with high-tech investment accelerating. That's slower than pre-pandemic trends but still solid enough to support consumer spending on vehicles in the world's largest auto market. The risk is that growth could slow further if Beijing's stimulus falters, directly hitting demand for both domestic and foreign-branded cars.

The Trump administration revived Section 338 authority and slapped a 50 percent tariff on Canadian products, using the trade tool as leverage in renegotiating the USMCA. For automakers, this threatens the integrated North American supply chain—engines and parts flow constantly across the U.S.-Canada border. A prolonged trade war could force expensive production reshuffling. India also delayed its E25 ethanol fuel mandate by at least a year, removing near-term regulatory pressure on fuel systems but leaving the industry uncertain about when the requirement will actually take effect.

Watch whether the U.S. and Canada reach a new trade deal within the next 30 days before tariffs crimp cross-border sourcing.

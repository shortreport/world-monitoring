US and Japanese defense ministers have agreed to build a new fighter-jet missile production facility in Japan, a direct signal to China and North Korea. Beijing is simultaneously working to weaken US-Taiwan relations, and Pakistan has already denied landing rights to Taiwanese airlines, citing its one-China policy. Watch for Beijing's reaction to the Japan missile deal over the next 72 hours — it's likely to be framed as provocation tied to Taiwan.

The post-midterm direction of US China policy — hawkish continuation or recalibration — will determine whether these supply chain fractures deepen or stabilize.

Key Takeaways
2026-11-04 10:04 JST  ·  ~1 min read

Thailand will raise excise duties on imported battery EVs and range-extended EVs to 35% from January 2027, a direct response to surging Chinese EV imports. Indonesia and Malaysia may follow, but Thailand, ASEAN's largest auto production hub, has the most at stake.

European Parliament groups are moving to make EU corporate fleet electrification targets "aspirational" rather than binding, putting the Commission's mandatory EV quota plan in jeopardy. Lawmakers are also weighing a freeze on the PHEV utility factor calculation until 2029-2030, a win automakers have lobbied hard for.

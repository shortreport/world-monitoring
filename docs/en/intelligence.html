The EU-China hybrid deal caps imports at 350,000 vehicles in 2027, rising 6% annually, giving automakers like Stellantis, Renault and Volkswagen temporary relief from Chinese competition. But Chinese luxury brands are already bypassing these defenses by manufacturing in Europe through partners like Magna and Stellantis, directly challenging Mercedes, BMW and Porsche.

A center-right revolt over the Industrial Accelerator Act threatens EU efforts to protect automakers through procurement rules, risking delays or a weakened law amid WTO and trade-war concerns.

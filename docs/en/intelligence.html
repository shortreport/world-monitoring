Key Takeaways
2026-11-14 14:57 JST  ·  ~1 min read

UK-China EV tariff talks and a possible EU-UK tariff reprieve both center on the same risk: rules-of-origin rules threatening billions in auto trade. If the UK aligns with EU-style tariffs on Chinese EVs, it risks Chinese retaliation but avoids losing Jaguar Land Rover's £2.5-3.5bn EU market access.

Separately, the EU is considering letting 2026-spec batteries qualify under current rules, which could spare automakers from €1.5bn in 2026 tariffs, though 2027 treatment is still unresolved.

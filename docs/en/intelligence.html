Key Takeaways
2026-10-20 09:13 JST  ·  ~1 min read

EU policy is shifting to favor domestic and "partner" industrial capacity. Brussels will push AI industrialization in autos, healthcare, and defense through 2027, giving automakers access to support for AI-driven manufacturing, but fragmented member-state enforcement of the AI Act could complicate compliance.

Separately, the Industrial Accelerator Act keeps automotive provisions intact despite Franco-German efforts to remove them, and sets a six-month deadline for defining which countries qualify for "Made in Europe" treatment—a key sourcing and investment consideration for automakers.

Key Takeaways
2026-10-21 10:45 JST  ·  ~1 min read

EU lawmakers have agreed to soften the 2035 car CO2 target to an effective 87.5% cut, letting automakers offset part of the reduction through green steel investments rather than tailpipe cuts alone. The deal also freezes plug-in hybrid utility factors through 2030 and extends EV super-credits to larger "Made in Europe" models, giving automakers more flexibility while weakening the original near-total combustion ban.
